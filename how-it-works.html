How it works

Setup takes one call. After that the work runs on the same schedule every week, and you hear from us only when something needs your OK.

Getting started

  1. Step 1

    One 45-minute call

    We learn your services, prices, service area and how you talk to customers. You do this once.

  2. Step 2

    Add us as a manager

    On your Google profile, without your password. The accounts stay in your name, and you can remove us any time.

  3. Step 3

    We do the weekly work

    Posts, photos, review replies and messages. Anything about price, financing or a complaint waits for your OK.

  4. Step 4

    One page a month

    Calls from Google, new reviews, tune-ups booked. Then a 15-minute call to plan the next month.

A typical week

MondayWe collect new reviews, job photos and anything you flagged, then write the week's post and review replies.
TuesdayYou OK anything sensitive by text. We publish.
ThursdaySecond check for new reviews, so none sit unanswered.
FridayWe ask for photos from the week's jobs.
1st of the monthOne-page report and a 15-minute call.

What we need from you

  • The 45-minute call at the start
  • Manager access to your Google profile
  • A few job photos each week, sent by text
  • A quick yes or no when we send something for your OK

Tell us the one thing you want fixed

Twenty minutes on the phone. We look at your Google listing first and tell you what we would change, whether or not you hire us.
