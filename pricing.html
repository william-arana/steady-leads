What about ad spend?

Ads are paid by you, straight to Google and Meta, from accounts in your name. We never mark it up and never hold your money. Most shops spend $1,000 to $4,000 a month depending on size and season. On the first call we recommend a starting budget, treat the first 60 days as a test, then show you what each booked job actually cost.

What we promise

  • A new post every week and a reply to every review within two business days
  • Your report by the 5th of every month
  • Month to month after the first 90 days. Cancel any time
  • You own everything: your profile, your ad accounts, your customer list
  • Reviews: if you send our request after every job, you get the number of new Google reviews we agree on in 90 days, or we keep working for free until you do

What we will not promise: a number of leads, a #1 spot on Google, or a revenue figure. Nobody honest can, because they depend on things like who answers your phone and how hot the summer is.
